# Hook library

Phrases to look for in a transcript, weak openers to cut, and rewrites. Use them to spot hooks the source already has. Don't put words in a speaker's mouth: rewrites go in the on-screen `title`, or tell you where to start the cut.

## Trigger phrases by type

| Type | Listen for | Example line found in a source |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | "here's why", "the real reason", "nobody talks about", "turns out", "what happened next", "the secret" | "Here's the real reason our clips stopped at 10,000 views." |
| Contrarian | "everyone's wrong about", "stop doing", "unpopular opinion", "the truth is", "is dead", "overrated" | "Posting every day is the worst advice in this industry." |
| Specific question | "why does", "how did", "what if", "would you", "can it" + a concrete subject | "Can this model pass a real coding interview?" |
| Identity | "if you're a…", "if you run…", "for anyone who…" | "If you run a podcast and you're not clipping it, you're wasting it." |
| Surprising number | numbers with a unit: "$40k", "3 million views", "in 11 seconds", "10x" | "We made $40,000 from one 20-second clip." |
| Social proof | a named person or brand + a result | "My client went from 200 to 90,000 followers in a month." |
| Transformation | "before", "after", "used to", "now", "went from…to" | "Six months ago this took two developers two days." |
| Stakes | "lose", "cost", "almost quit", "broke", "fired", "last chance" | "One email almost cost us the whole company." |
| Mid-story drop | the tense sentence inside a story: "and then he looked at me and said…" | "And the investor just stood up and walked out." |
| Revelation | "I never told anyone", "honestly", "I was wrong", "behind the scenes" | "I've never said this on a podcast before." |
| Practical value | "how to", "the one thing", "do this", "the trick is" | "Cut the first sentence of every answer. That's the whole trick." |
| Sound (`sonic_drop`) | drop, chorus entry, crowd roar, a sudden silence | The first beat after the build-up |
| Picture (`visual_spectacle`) | a reveal, a crash, a transformation, an odd object | The finished product turning on |

## Weak openers: cut them, start later

| Opener | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "So…", "Um…", "Yeah, so…", "Okay…" | Filler; the viewer hears nothing in 1 s | Start on the next content word |
| "As I was saying…", "Like I said earlier…" | Callback to context the clip doesn't have | Start where the point is restated |
| "It…", "That…", "He…", "This…" | The viewer doesn't know what it is | Start one line earlier, or name it in the `title` |
| "Hey guys, welcome back…" | Greeting | Never a hook: cut |
| "This video is sponsored by…" | Sponsor read | Cut |
| "Great question." | Interviewer filler | Start on the answer; question in the `title` |
| A long question from the host | The hook is in the answer | Open on the answer |
| A logo, intro card or title screen | Nothing to stop for | Cut to the first face or action |

## Before and after

Each pair shows what the clip opened on, then the better cut with its text hook.

1. **Podcast.**
   Before: opens at "So yeah, I think, um, the thing about growth is…" (the point lands 12 s later).
   After: opens on "We grew faster when we posted less." Text: "Posting less doubled our growth".
2. **Founder interview.**
   Before: the host's 15 s question, then the answer.
   After: opens on the answer "We fired our biggest customer." Text: "Why we fired a $2M customer".
3. **Tutorial.**
   Before: "In this video I'll show you how to…"
   After: opens on the finished result for 2 s, then the steps. Text: "This render took 4 minutes, not 40".
4. **Stream.**
   Before: 20 s of gameplay, then the clutch play, then the reaction.
   After: opens on the streamer's scream (1 s), then the play from 5 s before it. Text: "1 HP, 3 enemies, no ammo".
5. **Music.**
   Before: the 25 s intro.
   After: opens on the drop, loops back into it at the end. Text: the song name and the hook line.
6. **AI demo.**
   Before: "So today we're testing a new model."
   After: opens on the output appearing. Text: "It wrote the whole app in 11 seconds".

## Text hook patterns (5–9 words)

- "Nobody tells you this about [X]"
- "[Common habit] is why [bad result]"
- "If you [identity], watch this"
- "[N] [people] made this mistake"
- "The [thing] that cost us [loss]"
- "Why [person] [surprising action]"
- "I tried [X] for [time]. Here's what happened"
- "[Before] → [after] in [time]"

Keep it true to the clip: the payoff must be in it.
